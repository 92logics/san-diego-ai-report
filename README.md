# San Diego County AI report writing (prototype)

Scripted sample by [92 Logics](https://www.92logics.com) for the County of San Diego Purchasing and Contracting solicitation on BuyNet, RFx BPM013365, Artificial Intelligence (AI) Report Writing Services, for the Department of Probation. It is not an official county system and not a bid.

## Buyer and notice

- Buyer: San Diego County — Purchasing and Contracting
- Notice: https://sdbuynet.sandiegocounty.gov/
- Contact email on the public HigherGov page and in the 92 Logics lead record: VanessaC.Rios@sdcounty.ca.gov

## What it shows

A labeled report-writing assistant. Answers and the sample report text are scripted from public pages (the BuyNet solicitation list, the CaliforniaBids notice page, and the HigherGov opportunity page). The sample packet is fictional. Every figure in a sample output is marked as a placeholder and is not County data. Questions and tasks those pages do not cover are refused. Nothing calls a live AI service.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No install and no build step. It works offline.
